Context & Need

Problem Statement & Motivation

Why Pakistani Sign Language requires dedicated artificial intelligence

🔴 1.5M+ Deaf Individuals

Over 1.5 million people in Pakistan live with profound hearing or speech impairment, encountering daily communication barriers in educational institutions, healthcare, and public offices.

🔴 ASL vs PSL Disparity

90%+ of existing academic research focuses on American Sign Language (ASL). PSL possesses completely different phonology, hand morphologies, and Urdu linguistic grammar.

🔴 Expensive Traditional Hardware

Glove-based sensory interfaces and wearable devices are prohibitively costly and cumbersome for day-to-day adoption.

🟢 Our Vision

A zero-cost, purely camera-driven, browser-accessible software application that translates PSL into Urdu text and natural voice in real time.

Scope & Goals

Project Objectives & Scope

  • Real-Time 3D Landmark Tracking: Client-side detection of 21 hand joints (63 spatial coordinates) at 30 fps via Google MediaPipe.
  • 51 PSL Class Classification: Covering 36 Urdu letters (حروفِ تہجی), 10 digits (۰ تا ۹), and 5 core conversational phrases (شکریہ، مدد، وغیرہ).
  • Deep Sequence Modeling: Implementing a Bidirectional LSTM neural network with sliding temporal windows and prediction smoothing.
  • Two-Way Output Modalities: Visual display in Urdu Nastaliq calligraphy plus native Urdu speech synthesis (TTS).
  • Administrative Analytics: Full administrative dashboard with 7-day trend visualizations, confidence metrics, and event audit logging.

Data Strategy

PSL Vocabulary & Dataset Representation

🔤 36 Urdu Letters

From Alif (ا) to Bari Yeh (ے), encompassing the entire Urdu alphabet for full fingerspelling capability.

🔢 10 Numerical Digits

Urdu numerals ۰ تا ۹ (0 to 9) for numerical values, phone numbers, and transactional queries.

💬 5 Daily Phrases

Essential expressions: السلام علیکم (Salam), شکریہ (Shukriya), ہاں (Yes), نہیں (No), مدد (Help).

Coordinate-Based Invariance: Unlike raw pixel images, normalized 3D landmark coordinates eliminate lighting sensitivity, complex background interference, and variations in skin tone.
Machine Learning

Bidirectional LSTM Model Architecture

  • Input Tensor: Shape (30, 63) — 30 sequential frames × 21 landmarks × 3 coordinates.
  • Bi-LSTM Layer 1: 128 units (bidirectional) with sequence returns to capture full gesture context.
  • Regularization: Batch Normalization + Dropout (0.25) to prevent memorization and overfitting.
  • Bi-LSTM Layer 2: 64 units capturing high-level temporal transitions.
  • Dense Layers: 128 units (ReLU) ➔ Batch Normalization ➔ 64 units (ReLU).
  • Softmax Output: 51 units yielding class probabilities for all PSL signs.
Stability Engineering

Anti-Flicker & Prediction Smoothing

⚠️ The Jitter Problem

Micro-movements in fingers, lighting changes, or brief occlusion can cause instant frame-to-frame prediction flipping, ruining user experience.

✅ Circular Majority Voting

Our custom SignPredictor evaluates a circular buffer of the past 7 frames using statistical mode voting:
prediction = mode([y_t-6, ..., y_t])

🎯 Confidence Threshold Gating

Signs are only logged and enunciated when smoothed confidence exceeds 75%, ensuring 100% stable presentation to the user.

Engineering Obstacles

Challenges Faced & Solutions

  • No Pre-existing PSL Dataset: Built custom OpenCV + MediaPipe collection utility (`collect_data.py`) to systematically capture coordinate sequence arrays.
  • Prediction Jitter & Hand Tremors: Designed sliding temporal window buffer with majority voting and threshold gating.
  • Urdu Audio Speech Engine: Integrated browser Web Speech Synthesis with targeted Urdu locale prioritization and phonetic fallback cues.
  • Video Bandwidth Overload: Extracted landmarks client-side via WebAssembly, streaming only 63 coordinates rather than raw video frames.
